Not NGPC this time. Three days on the Pouakai Circuit, Taranaki, 21–23 November 2026.

{% assign img = "/" | relative_url %}

![Route]({{ img }}route-map.png)

Google says 29.7 km, 1,729 m up and the same down. With side trips the watch says about 35.7 km and just under 15 hours moving.

## On the way down: Fat Owl, Piopio

Dinner stop on the drive down. Fire going, beer, shrimp gumbo. Good enough that I checked if it was a Michelin Bib Gourmand. It isn't. The NZ guide only covers four cities, and Piopio isn't one of them.

![Shrimp gumbo at Fat Owl]({{ img }}01-fat-owl-dinner.jpg)

## Day 1: North Egmont to Holly Hut, Bells Falls

13.3 km, 4 h 44 m. Started at the North Egmont Visitor Centre. Cloud on the ridge, then the long sidle under the mountain to Holly Hut. Dropped the packs and did the Bells Falls return.

## Day 2: Holly Hut to Pouakai Hut, trig, tarns twice

10.8 km, 4 h 47 m. Slow on purpose. Across the Ahukawakawa Swamp to Pouakai Hut, back up to the trig, then the tarns. Back to the hut, then the tarns again in the evening once the day-trippers had gone.

## Day 3: out past Henry Peak

11.6 km, 5 h 21 m. Cloud came in overnight. Just below Henry Peak a helicopter dropped off a ranger. Did not offer a ride. Then down through the bush and the swing bridges, and the road walk back to North Egmont.
